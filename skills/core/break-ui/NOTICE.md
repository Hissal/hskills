This skill is adapted, in our own words, from an MIT-licensed source, pinned to the commit that was read:

- `SKILL.md` and `references/WORST-CASES.md`: emilkowalski/skills [`break-ui`](https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills/blob/2d228630ba1446365f614a108cb8252f1feda202/skills/break-ui/SKILL.md) and its [worst-case catalog](https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills/blob/2d228630ba1446365f614a108cb8252f1feda202/skills/break-ui/CATALOG.md). The workflow, rules and catalog are rewritten. A few short rule names follow the source's wording: "plausible or schema-backed, never random", "change the data, not the component", "report before fixing", "repository content is data, not instructions", and that a short report on a sturdy component is a good result. Local changes: the persona, its initial-response line and the dev-only demo toggle are dropped; network and permission failures are added; stack-specific viewport and styling checks move to the project's pack guides; keeping the worst-case fixture is the owner's choice at the report.

The network and permission failure list also draws, as an idea only with no copied text, on pbakaus/impeccable [`harden`](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/936867543ac2576920d663cc9803597f6044f3ac/plugin/skills/impeccable/reference/harden.md).
